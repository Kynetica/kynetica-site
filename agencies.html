How it works

  1. You send us the client's website and a name/email for the owner.
  2. We research the site, industry, and public footprint, then send the owner a 10-minute questionnaire (dispatch process, current tools, biggest headache).
  3. We write the audit — 6–10 pages, branded to your spec — and deliver it to you within 48 hours of the questionnaire coming back.
  4. You hand it to the client, resell it, or use it to open the upsell conversation for your care plan.

What we honestly can't see from outside

We research the public site, reviews, booking flow, and whatever the owner tells us in the questionnaire. We are not inside their CRM, dispatch software, phone system, or accounting — so the audit won't catch things like a misconfigured internal routing rule, a broken integration between two backend tools, or exact current labor costs unless the owner shares them. The questionnaire is deliberately short (10 minutes) because most of what we need is visible from outside or is a quick owner answer, not a systems audit. If a client needs a deep technical audit of their internal stack, say so up front — that's a different (and bigger) engagement, not this one.
