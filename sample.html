3. Findings, ranked

3.1 Intake form re-entry — 9 hrs/wk

What happens now. New patients fill a PDF or paper form; the front desk types it into the PMS. At ~40 new patients/week and ~12 minutes each, that is 8–9 hours.

What to do. Digital intake that writes directly to the PMS. If you're on Dentrix, Eaglesoft or Open Dental, the native or integrated e-forms (Dentrix Patient Forms, Yapi, Modento) eliminate the re-entry step entirely.

  • Cost: $150–300/month
  • Setup: 1–2 weeks, mostly configuring the form
  • Risk: low; falls back to paper if the tablet dies

3.2 Appointment reminders — 8 hrs/wk

What happens now. Staff phone or hand-text the next day's schedule.

What to do. Automated two-way SMS/email reminders with confirm/cancel, synced to the PMS schedule. Most PMS vendors bundle this; standalone options include Weave, RevenueWell and Lighthouse 360. Expect no-shows to drop 20–30% as a side effect — that is usually worth more than the labour saved.

  • Cost: $200–350/month (often bundled)
  • Setup: days, not weeks
  • Risk: low; keep a human on exceptions (same-day cancellations, complex reschedules)

3.3 Insurance eligibility checks — 7 hrs/wk

What happens now. Manual portal look-ups or phone calls the day before each visit.

What to do. Batch eligibility verification that runs automatically 48h ahead of every appointment (Vyne Dental, Zuub, or your PMS's integrated clearinghouse). Staff review only the exceptions.

  • Cost: ~$0.30–0.60 per check, or a flat monthly plan
  • Setup: 1–3 weeks including payer mapping
  • Risk: medium; a few payers still need a phone call — the exception list handles them

3.4 – 3.7 Next and Later

  • Recall / reactivation outreach (4 hrs/wk). Rules-based campaigns from the PMS recall list — patients overdue 6, 12 and 18 months get a different message. Usually a setting in the reminder tool you chose in 3.2.
  • Review requests (1.5 hrs/wk). Trigger a review SMS on checkout for completed appointments; suppress for patients flagged as unhappy. Same tool again.
  • Supply reordering (1 hr/wk). Par-level reordering through your supplier's portal or a lightweight inventory app; only worth it once the front-desk items are done.
  • End-of-day reconciliation (0.5 hr/wk). Export PMS day-sheet to the accounting system automatically. Low hours, but it also removes an error source.

4. Thirty-day plan

  1. Week 1 — pick the reminder tool (fastest win, lowest risk). Turn it on for one location.
  2. Week 2 — configure digital intake; run it in parallel with paper for one week.
  3. Week 3 — enable batch eligibility; define the exception workflow.
  4. Week 4 — measure: front-desk hours on the three tasks, no-show rate, and errors. Compare against the numbers in section 1.

5. Assumptions behind the numbers

  • Loaded front-desk cost: $22/hour (wage + payroll taxes + benefits). Change this and every dollar figure scales linearly.
  • ~40 new patients/week, ~180 total appointments/week across both locations, based on published hours and chair count.
  • Software costs are list prices as of September 2026; most vendors discount for multi-location practices.
