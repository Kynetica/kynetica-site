2. How I looked

Sources

I read the site pages /, /contact-us, /schedule-service, /services plus the nine answers, the task and the five checkout numbers; I did not have access to any internal system.

What the website shows. The home page and schedule-service page both show an online booking or request button and advertise 24/7 emergency service with a phone number on every page. The contact form on contact-us has no visible destination. No Google or Yelp reviews link or text-us option appears on the pages read.

What I could not see: exact part correction time per job; exact number of after-hours texts per week; exact number of unmatched payments per week.

3. The five, ranked

3.1 · Move off paper tickets to Jobber with QuickBooks Online sync · 6 to 12 hours a week

You told me every job gets written on a paper ticket in the truck then your wife re-types it into QuickBooks at night to invoice.

What to do. Switch the whole crew to Jobber so the job form on the phone replaces the paper ticket, the price book replaces typed part numbers, and the QuickBooks Online sync replaces night-time re-typing. The website booking widget and automatic review requests come included in the same subscription at no extra cost.

Which tool, and why.

- Jobber, about $49 to $199 a month: Cheapest full platform with job form, price book, invoice-on-completion, reminders, on-my-way texts, review requests and QuickBooks Online sync in one app for 3-9 tech shops moving off paper

Where the traps are.

- Techs resist the phone at first if the price book is not loaded before rollout

Hours and dollars. You told me 45 jobs a week and paper tickets with night re-typing; at eight to fifteen minutes per job that is 6 to 12 hours. $9,000 to $18,000 a year back against $588 to $2,388 a year in software. Estimate until your numbers replace mine.
