Example result (illustrative). Not a real customer, shown to demonstrate the format.

Named task: "Every evening I re-type the day's jobs from paper tickets into our invoicing software."

Decomposed: (1) tech writes ticket on paper at the job, (2) ticket physically returns to the office, (3) office re-types the job into invoicing software, (4) invoice gets emailed to the customer.

Automatable steps: step 1 is automatable with a form on a phone (a booking/CRM tool category many shops already run); step 3 is automatable with the same tool if it can generate an invoice directly from the completed job form, removing the re-type entirely.

Setup effort (estimate): roughly 2-4 hours to set up the form and connect it to invoicing, depending on what's already in place.

Hours recovered (estimate): roughly 3-6 hours a week, depending on job volume. This is a guess from nine answers, not a measurement of your business.

Two more leaks: scheduling replies happen by hand at all hours; supplier orders get chased by phone instead of a standing reorder.
