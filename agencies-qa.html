What we check, every week

  • Quote / estimate forms — do they submit, does the confirmation show, does the lead actually land somewhere?
  • Booking handoffs — scheduling widgets, "book now" flows, third-party booking embeds.
  • tel: links — do they fire a real call on mobile, is the number correct and current?
  • Contact details — address, hours, email links, anything a lead needs to reach the business.
  • Mobile and desktop — tested on both, because a broken mobile tap target is a broken lead.

What you get each week

  1. A pass over every path above, on each client site, with screenshots and evidence for anything broken or degraded.
  2. A prioritized repair ticket list — what's actually costing leads first, what's cosmetic.
  3. A retest after your team (or your client's) ships a fix, so you know it actually worked.

What this is not

Not SEO. We don't touch rankings, keywords, or content strategy.

Not security testing. We're not probing for vulnerabilities, doing penetration tests, or auditing infrastructure. If we notice something that looks like a security issue in the course of testing lead paths, we'll flag it — but that's not the service.

No revenue guarantees. Fixing a broken form gets a lead to land instead of vanishing; it doesn't promise a dollar amount of new revenue. We report what's broken and what we verified fixed — the business outcome depends on everything downstream of the lead.
